Our Story

Boreal & Bean was born from a deep love for Canada's northern wilderness. Our founders were inspired by the crisp morning air, the scent of damp earth, and the quiet beauty of forest landscapes.

Although coffee beans cannot grow in Canada's cold winters, the feeling of the coffee experience can still be connected to the Canadian landscape. We source high-quality beans from sustainable farms and roast them with care in Ontario.

Every part of Boreal & Bean reflects our respect for nature — from wild-inspired flavours to reclaimed Canadian wood in our café interior. Our goal is to create a warm café experience rooted in sustainability, craft, and slow living.

Core Values

Canadian Craftsmanship

Our café space uses reclaimed Canadian wood and locally made design elements to create a warm, natural atmosphere.

Green Mission

We use compostable packaging and support tree-planting projects connected to Canadian forests.

Wild-Inspired Flavours

Our drinks use ingredients such as maple syrup, wildflower honey, birch syrup, and spruce tips.
